Legal

Terms of engagement, written to be read.

What this covers

These terms govern the use of vishvutech.com and the general shape of professional engagements by Vishvu Tech (independent software engineering services, Bengaluru, India). Project-specific agreements always take precedence over this page — nothing here overrides a signed contract.

The working model

Engagements are fixed-scope with fixed pricing wherever requirements are testable, following a paid discovery sprint when the scope needs sharpening. Weekly demos are part of every project: "on track" should be a verifiable statement, not a mood. Intellectual property transfers fully to the client upon final payment — repositories, credentials, and documentation included.

Site content & honest numbers

Case studies describe real engagements with details generalized where client confidentiality applies. Metrics are reported as measured at the time of writing; the CONTENT-GUIDE in this site's repository tracks every published figure to its source. Nothing on this site is a guarantee of identical results — your checkout, clinic, or field crew is, regrettably, your own.

Availability & warranty

Delivered projects include a 30-day warranty against defects in the delivered code. Ongoing support is a separate retainer, never a hostage situation. This website itself is provided as-is — though I'd argue the uptime record speaks for itself.

Contact & disputes

Everything starts — and ideally ends — with a conversation at hello@vishvutech.com. Disputes attempt good-faith resolution first; jurisdiction is Bengaluru, India.
