Privacy, in plain language.

The short version

No cookies. No advertising trackers. No data brokers. The analytics on this site are self-hosted, cookieless, and aggregate — they tell me which pages get read, not who read them.

What the contact form collects

If you send an inquiry, I receive your name, email address, and whatever you chose to tell me about the project (type, budget band, timeline, and your one-line pitch). This is stored to respond to you and to reference the project if we work together. It is retained for 24 months, then deleted. It is never sold, shared, or used for marketing sequences — you'll get a reply, not a drip campaign.

What the server logs

Standard web-server access logs (IP address, request, timestamps) rotate out within 14 days and exist purely for security and debugging. Analytics events are anonymous usage signals (page, referrer, device class) with no cross-site identifiers.

Your rights

You can ask, at any time, what inquiry data exists about you and request its deletion — a single email to hello@vishvutech.com handles it, answered within 72 hours. GDPR and India DPDP requests included, no forms required.

Third parties

This site loads zero third-party scripts. Fonts, analytics, and assets are all served from this site's own infrastructure. The only external requests happen when you click an outbound link (GitHub, LinkedIn, WhatsApp) — at which point their policies apply, not mine.
