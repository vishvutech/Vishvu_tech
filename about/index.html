03 — The maker

One senior engineer. Studio-grade delivery.

No account managers, no handoffs, no telephone game. The person who scopes your project is the person who designs it, builds it, and answers for it in production.

The journey · since 2019

I'm Vishvu — the engineer behind Vishvu Tech. Since 2019 I've designed, built, and shipped fourteen-plus products across web, mobile, and AI: checkout systems that lift revenue, field apps that survive dead zones, dashboards that turned six hours of weekly reporting into a Monday message people actually read.

Before going independent I worked on products where "later" meant "after the outage" — which taught me the two habits clients mention first: I instrument everything so decisions have data, and I write runbooks so nothing depends on remembering.

The studio model is deliberate. One senior engineer accountable end-to-end, plus a trusted network of specialists — design, content, QA — assembled per project when the scope earns them. You get agency-range capability without agency fog, and a schedule that says "no" honestly instead of "yes" optimistically.

Timeline

Six years, one throughline.

2019 — First shipped products→ Two e-commerce builds for local retailers. Learned that launch day is the beginning of the work.
2021 — The specialization→ Went independent full-time. First offline-first mobile app; the sync engine that became FieldOps' DNA.
2023 — The systems era→ Design systems, accessibility-first rebuilds, and the ClinicFlow engagement that proved specs beat hero shots.
2024 — Field + clinic→ FieldOps and ClinicFlow shipped. The portfolio's decision-trail format became the standard for every engagement.
2025 — The AI discipline→ InsightLoop: rules where they're certain, models where they pay, humans where it matters. Also: this site, on a $0 stack.
