Do you work with existing codebases? +

Yes — audit first, then a scoped plan. I'll tell you plainly if a rebuild is cheaper than a rescue; sometimes the honest answer is "three fixes, not three months."

Who owns the code? +

You do, fully, on final payment. Repos transfer to your organization; nothing stays hostage in my accounts.

Can you take over after launch? +

Every project ships with a runbook, monitoring, and a 30-day warranty. After that: monthly retainer if you want it, clean handover if you don't.

Will an LLM hallucinate our numbers? +

Not on my watch — arithmetic runs through deterministic code; the model narrates what that code computed. Every generated sentence logs its source numbers.

What about our data privacy? +

Pipelines run against your own infrastructure where possible; nothing customer-facing sends itself without a human click until you say otherwise.
